1. Introduction

Equal Opportunity Cell (EOC) is established to ensure equality, inclusiveness, and fairness
among all students and staff members irrespective of gender, caste, religion, language,
disability, socio-economic background, or any other status. The cell aims to create a healthy
academic environment that promotes equal access to opportunities and prevents
discrimination within the institution.

The Equal Opportunity Cell works towards maintaining harmony, dignity, and respect
among students and employees while encouraging participation in academic, cultural,
social, and co-curricular activities. The cell also supports disadvantaged and
underrepresented groups by creating awareness about rights, responsibilities, and
institutional support systems.

2. Purpose
£
The purpose of the Equal Opportunity Cell is to:

e Promote equality and inclusiveness within the institution.
e Ensure fair treatment and equal opportunities for all students and staff.
e Prevent discrimination and harassment based on caste, gender, religion, disability, or

socio-economic status.
e Create awareness regarding constitutional values, human rights, and social justice.

e Support students belonging to weaker and marginalized sections.
Encourage participation of all students in academic and extracurricular activities.

3. Objectives of Equal Opportunity Cell
The primary objectives of the Equal Opportunity Cell are:
1. To ensure equal access to educational opportunities for all students.

2. To create awareness about equality, social justice, and constitutional rights.

3. To promote an inclusive and discrimination-free campus environment.

4. To encourage participation of students from diverse backgrounds.

6. Code of Conduct
Parivarthana Business School is committed to providing equal opportunities and
maintaining a respectful and inclusive environment for all students and staff members. The

institution strictly prohibits discrimination based on gender, caste, religion, disability,
language, ethnicity, or socio-economic background.

All members of the institution are expected to:

° Treat every individual with dignity and respect.

° Promote equality and inclusiveness in all academic and non-academic activities.
° Avoid discriminatory language, behavior, or practices.

e Encourage mutual understanding and harmony among students and staff.

. Support institutional policies related to equality and social justice.

. Maintain confidentiality while addressing grievances and complaints.

The institution shall take necessary action against any act of discrimination, harassment, or
violation of Equal Opportunity Cell policies.

Durat 1 Validit
This MoU shall remain valid for a period of three (3) years from the date of signing.
It may be renewed or extended upon mutual agreement of both parties. Either party

may terminate the MoU with 30 days’ prior written notice to the other party.

Confidentiality Clause
Both parties agree to maintain confidentiality regarding any proprietary or sensitive

information shared during the course of this collaboration. No confidential

information shall be disclosed to a third party without prior written consent.

Financial Considerations

This MoU is a non-financial agreement. However, if any financial arrangements arise

in the future, they shall be mutually discussed and agreed upon in writing.

Dispute Resolution
Any dispute arising out of the interpretation or implementation of this MoU shall be
resolved amicably through mutual discussions. In case of unresolved disputes,

jurisdiction will lie with the courts of Mysuru, Karnataka.

Amendment Clause

This MoU may be amended or modified only by mutual consent and written

agreement of both parties.

Standard Operating Procedure
Introduction:
Special rights have been conferred by the Indian Constitution to address the
social, educational and economic backwardness of Scheduled Castes & Scheduled Tribes

arising out of age-old social practices. To protect the rights of the students and prevent

any further discrimination and exploitation, Parivarthana Business School constituted

SC/ST Cell. The SC/ST cell of the college was established to empower the SC/ST
students in the college. The college takes special interest in facilitating financial
support to students from these communities from government agencies and other

sources. As per AICTE guidelines, a committee is formed for prevention of atrocities

against SC/ST students under the Act No. 33 of the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled
Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989. In case of any grievance in this regard,
students can approach the committee for redressal. The committee will suggest

measures to prevent atrocities, if any against any of the SC/ST students in the institute

and to ensure the feeling of security amongst them as per the provisionsin the said act.

Objectives:
The primary objective of creating this SC/ST cell in Parivarthana Business
School is to clearly demarcate the course of action of various government functionaries

- police, revenue, judiciary, welfare - across the hierarchy with relevant timelines,

wheréver applicable, for ensuring effective coordination and implementation of the
Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and the
Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Rules, 1995 - both

in letter and in spirit.

The SC/ST Cell is prepared with the following objectives:
(1) Guide the SC/ST students of the Institute, to optimally utilize the benefits of the
schemes offered by the Karnataka Governments, Government of India (GOl).

(2) Assist the staff of the SC/ST Cell and the Standing Committee in carrying out
their functions.

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP):
(1) The reservation policy of the Government pertaining to the admission of
students in Higher Education Institutions in Karnataka is strictly followed during
the admission of the graduate and postgraduate students in Parivarthana
Business School.

(2) After the admission the cell gets the data regarding the number of students

coming from the SC/ST quota from the college ofice.
(3) The college makes sure that all the SC/ST students are aware of the various

scholarships and benefits of the Government schemes like SC/ST Scholarship etc.
(4) The SC/ST Cell of the college makes sure that the students can avail the

scholarships without any problems. After the studentssubmit their applications
the concerned nonteaching staff looks after the matter and addresses all the
issues regarding the same.

(5) The college strives to create an atmosphere where no student from the backward

classes is bullied or harassed. If any instance of harassment is found or reported,

the SC/ST Celltakes necessary action with the help of the anti-ragging cell of the
college.

(6) Those who are unable to get their SC/ST Certificates, the cell co-operates with
them to complete the due procedure to receive their certificates.

(7) The cell also works towards the awareness of the students about the various
career options, guidance facilities and provides the students with the relevant

information pertaining to higher education, competitive exams for various govt.
jobs etc.

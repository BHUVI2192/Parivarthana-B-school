STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

INTERNAL COMPLAINTS COMMITTEE (ICC) / POSH COMMITTEE
1. Purpose

The Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the operational framework
for the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) / POSH Committee at
Parivarthana Business School. The Committee is established to provide a safe,
respectful and gender-sensitive environment, prevent and address sexual
harassment, provide a confidential grievance redressal mechanism, conduct
awareness programmes, and support compliance with the applicable
Prevention of Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace framework.

2. Scope
This SOP applies to:

-Women employees and faculty members

- Non-teaching and administrative staff

- Students

- Interns, trainees and other persons associated with the institution

- Contractual/temporary personnel and other persons covered by applicable
institutional policy

- ICC/POSH Committee members and institutional authorities

3. Objectives

The ICC / POSH Committee shall:

1. Prevent and prohibit sexual harassment in the institutional /workplace
environment.

2. Provide an accessible and confidential mechanism for receiving
complaints.

3. Ensure fair, impartial and time-bound handling of complaints.

4. Protect the dignity, safety and privacy of persons approaching the
Committee.

5. Conduct awareness and gender-sensitization programmes.

12. Confidentiality

‘ All complaints, inquiry proceedings, evidence and related records shall be
treated as confidential.

or required by applicable law.

‘ Committee members shall maintain impartiality, fairness and professional
conduct.

‘Records shall be stored securely with access restricted to authorized persons.
